🌲 Living in Finland: Domestic Hunting Framework & Fast-Track
If you reside in Finland, you participate under the full domestic system: you register with your local Game Management Association (RHY) using your Finnish personal ID (henkilötunnus), authenticate via Suomi.fi online banking, take the Hunter's Exam in English, and apply for standard police firearm permits (with military service requirements waived for non-citizens). There is no need for tourist passes—you have direct access to over 9 million hectares of Metsähallitus state lands and local hunting clubs from day one.
