Personalized Pathway Finder

Answer 4 quick questions to generate your exact legal roadmap, estimated costs, and required permits.

1. What is your residency status?
🇫🇮

Living in Finland

You have a Finnish address / personal ID code. Standard path through local Game Management Association (RHY).

🌍

Foreign Visitor / Tourist

You hold a hunting license in your home country or plan a temporary visit to hunt with an outfitter.

2. How do you want to hunt?
🔫

Traditional Firearms

Shotgun (birds/hare) or .308 rifle (deer/moose). Requires police permit & safe storage.

🏹

Compound Bow (Archery)

No police firearm permit or gun safe required! Quiet, ethical, ideal for suburban deer management.

🤝

Mentored / Guided (No Gun Ownership)

Shoot under immediate supervision (Valvottu ammunta) or apply for a Parallel Permit for a mentor's weapon.

3. What game do you want to pursue first?
🦆

Small Game & Birds

Forest grouse, waterfowl, wood pigeon, and hares. Accessible on public state lands via Eräluvat.fi.

🦌

Big Game (Deer & Moose)

High-seat deer ambush or club moose drives. Requires shooting test & club membership/quota.

🌲

All-Round Hunter

From August waterfowl opening to winter deer and moose drives across Finland.
