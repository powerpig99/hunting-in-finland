# 08. Finnish-English Hunting Glossary & Field Vocabulary

Whether communicating over a VHF hunting radio in a moose drive line, ordering ammunition at a sporting store, or speaking with your local game association coordinator, knowing core Finnish terminology is essential.

---

## 🏛️ Legal & Administrative Terms

| Finnish Term | Literal / English Meaning | Context & Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Metsästäjätutkinto** | Hunter's Examination | The 60-question qualifying test |
| **Riistanhoitomaksu** | Game Management Fee | The annual statutory fee (~43 €) |
| **Metsästyskortti** | Hunting Card | Proof of active license + hunter's insurance |
| **Oma riista** | "My Game" | Official digital registry portal & mobile app |
| **Riistanhoitoyhdistys (RHY)** | Game Management Association | Local statutory hunting authority (~280 nationwide) |
| **Suomen riistakeskus** | Finnish Wildlife Agency | National wildlife authority |
| **Metsästysseura** | Hunting Club | Local cooperative leasing private hunting lands |
| **Eräluvat** | Wilderness Permits | Metsähallitus portal for state land permits |
| **Hankkimislupa** | Firearm Acquisition Permit | Police authorization to buy a specific gun |
| **Hallussapitolupa** | Firearm Possession Permit | Permanent registration card for an owned gun |
| **Rinnakkaislupa** | Parallel Permit | Permit to co-use another person's firearm |
| **Valvottu ammunta** | Supervised Shooting | Shooting under the immediate eye of a permit holder |
| **Ampumakoe** | Shooting Test | 75 m test required for rifle hunting of large game |
| **Jousiampumakoe** | Bow Shooting Test | 18 m test required for bow hunting deer/boar |
| **Jokamiehenoikeus** | Everyman's Right | Public freedom to roam (excludes hunting/fishing) |

## 🔫 Weapons & Equipment

| Finnish Term | English Equivalent | Context & Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Haulikko** | Shotgun | Typically 12-gauge (*12/76*) over-and-under |
| **Kivääri** | Rifle | Bolt-action (*pulttilukkoinen*) in .308 Win, etc. |
| **Yhdistelmäase / Rihlakko** | Combination gun | Shotgun barrel + rifle barrel combined |
| **Taljajousi** | Compound bow | Uses cams and pulleys for high efficiency |
| **Vastakaarijousi** | Recurve bow | Traditional archery bow |
| **Asekaappi** | Gun safe | Must meet EN 14450 S1 if owning 5+ firearms |
| **Äänenvaimennin** | Suppressor / Silencer | Widely permitted and encouraged for hearing protection |
| **Kiikaritähtäin** | Rifle scope | Magnified optic for rifles |
| **Patruuna** | Cartridge / Ammunition | Bullet or shot shell |
| **Hauli** | Shot pellet | Steel (*teräshauli*) or lead (*lyijyhauli*) |
| **Luoti** | Bullet | Solid copper (*kupariluoti*) or jacketed |
| **Nuoli** | Arrow | Carbon or aluminum shaft |
| **Leikkaava kärki** | Cutting broadhead | Must be $\ge 22\text{ mm}$ width for deer |
| **Puukko** | Traditional Nordic belt knife | Fixed-blade knife used for field dressing & bushcraft |
| **Oranssi huomioväri** | Blaze orange safety color | Legally required 2/3 coverage for big game hunts |
| **Metsästysradio** | VHF hunting radio | Operates on Finnish hunting frequencies (68–72 MHz) |

## 🌲 Hunting Methods & Actions

| Finnish Term | English Equivalent | Context & Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Seurajahti** | Club / syndicate hunt | Group hunt organized by a hunting club |
| **Passimies / Passipaikka** | Stander / Stand location | Hunter waiting stationary at an assigned perimeter spot |
| **Ajomies / Ajoketju** | Beater / Drive line | Walking through timber pushing game toward standers |
| **Kyttäysjahti / Kytis** | High-seat / Blind hunting | Ambushing game from an elevated tower at feeding sites |
| **Pystykorvajahti** | Barking spitz hunting | Hunting capercaillie or grouse with Finnish Spitz dog |
| **Haukku** | Dog's bark | Indicates dog has located and held bird or moose |
| **Houkuttelupyynti** | Calling / Decoy hunting | Whistling for hazel grouse or using duck decoys |
| **Ampumasektori** | Safe shooting sector | Strict angular zone where a stander may discharge |
| **Turvallinen tausta** | Safe backstop | Solid ground or berm behind the target |
| **Nuotio** | Campfire | The sacred social gathering spot during hunt breaks |
| **Makkaranpaisto** | Sausage grilling | Essential tradition of every Finnish hunt |

## 🔪 Game Handling & Meat Preparation

| Finnish Term | English Equivalent | Context & Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Saalis** | Harvest / Bag / Catch | The taken animal |
| **Pistäminen** | Bleeding the carcass | Severing throat blood vessels right after kill |
| **Suolistus** | Field dressing / Gutting | Removing entrails, stomach, and digestive tract |
| **Nylkeminen** | Skinning | Removing the hide |
| **Lahtivaja** | Slaughterhouse / Butchery shed | Club facility for hanging and skinning big game |
| **Riiputus / Raakakypsytys** | Aging / Hanging meat | Hanging carcass at 2–6°C to tenderize venison |
| **Lihaosuus** | Meat share | Hunter's portion of butchered meat in a club hunt |
| **Trikiinitutkimus** | Trichinella parasite test | Mandatory lab test for wild boar and bear meat |

## 📻 Critical Radio Commands You Will Hear in the Field

- **"Passit paikalla!"** — *"Standers are in position!"* All perimeter hunters are set; weapons may now be loaded in the stand.
- **"Ajo alkaa!"** — *"The drive begins!"* Beaters and dogs have entered the forest block. Maintain extreme sector discipline.
- **"Paukku lähti!"** — *"A shot was fired!"* A hunter reports they discharged a round; standers stay in place and wait.
- **"Hirvi nurin / Peura nurin!"** — *"Moose is down / Deer is down!"* Target has fallen safely.
- **"Jahti poikki, aseet tyhjäksi!"** — *"Hunt is halted, unload all weapons!"* Clear the chamber and leave weapon open. Movement allowed.
