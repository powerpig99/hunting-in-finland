09. Booking a Shooting Session & Joining a Hunting Club

Section 88 supervised shooting without a gun permit, specific Finnish ranges, RHY coordinator outreach, and copy-paste email templates in Finnish & English.

🎯 Part 1: Where & How to Book a Shooting Session (Zero License Needed)

Under Section 88 of the Finnish Firearms Act (Ampuma-aselaki), any person can legally shoot a firearm at an approved range under the immediate supervision of a licensed firearm owner or instructor. You do not need a firearm permit, and you do not need to have taken the Hunter's Exam yet!

🌲 Part 2: Where & How to Join a Hunting Club (Metsästysseura)

Over 80% of private forest land in Southern and Central Finland is leased by local hunting clubs (metsästysseurat), controlling 2,000 to 20,000+ hectares. Because Finnish law requires at least 1,000 hectares of contiguous land to issue a moose hunting license, joining a club is the essential pathway to hunting moose and deer.

🔍 Step 1: Find Municipal Clubs via Riista.fi

  1. Visit the official directory at riista.fi/yhteystiedot.
  2. Select your game district (e.g. Uusimaa) and click your home municipality (e.g. Espoo-Kauniainen, Kirkkonummi, Sipoo, Lohja, Vihti).
  3. Locate the Executive Coordinator (*Toiminnanohjaaja*). They maintain the official list of active local hunting clubs and know which ones are actively welcoming new members!

🤝 Step 2: The "Trojan Horse" Strategy for Newcomers

  • Volunteer for Summer Talkoot (Work Bees): Offer a day of carpentry or trail clearing to build high seats (passitornit) or haul mineral salt licks. Hard workers earn immediate respect.
  • Join Autumn Hunts as an Unarmed Beater (*Ajomies*): Volunteer to walk the drive lines in October/November moose and deer hunts wearing an orange vest. No gun, gun license, or hunting card required! You get to observe the hounds, learn the forest, share campfire meals, and earn the 2 club sponsors (suosittelijat) required for your formal membership application.

💳 Typical Financial Dues in a Finnish Hunting Club

  • Initiation Fee (*Liittymismaksu*): 100 € – 400 € (one-off payment upon formal election).
  • Annual Membership (*Vuosimaksu*): 50 € – 150 € / year (covers land leases and club facilities).
  • Big Game Syndicate Fee (*Hirvi-/peuramaksu*): 50 € – 200 € / season (yields ~20–60 kg of prime organic venison or moose meat).
