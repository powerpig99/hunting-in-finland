# 03. Bow Hunting (*Jousimetsästys*) — The Modern Entry Route

For beginners living in Finnish cities or suburban areas, **bow hunting** (*jousimetsästys*) is one of the most accessible, rewarding, and rapidly growing pathways into Finnish game harvesting. 

Unlike firearms, hunting bows are not classified as controlled weapons under the Finnish Firearms Act, eliminating months of police paperwork, background interviews, and heavy gun safe investments.

---

## 🌟 Why Bow Hunting Is Thriving in Finland

1. **Zero Police Bureaucracy**: You do not need an acquisition permit from the police, you do not need a police interview, and you do not need an approved steel safe in your apartment.
2. **Suburban Deer Abundance**: In Southern Finland (Uusimaa, Varsinais-Suomi, Kanta-Häme, Pirkanmaa), populations of invasive **white-tailed deer** (*valkohäntäpeura*) and native **roe deer** (*metsäkauris*) are extremely dense. Because rifle shooting is prohibited within 150 meters of residential buildings without landowner consent, hunting clubs actively recruit bow hunters to manage deer herds near inhabited areas safely and silently.
3. **Purity of the Hunt**: Bow hunting requires getting within 10–25 meters of the animal. It demands deep understanding of wind direction, animal behavior, camouflage, scent masking, and patience.
4. **Practice Anywhere**: You can legally shoot an archery target in a suitable backyard, designated archery range, or forest course without traveling to a distant certified ballistic shooting range.

---

## ⚖️ Legal Regulations & Equipment Requirements

Bow hunting is strictly governed by the Finnish Hunting Decree (*Metsästysasetus*, Section 20).

### 1. Permitted Bow Types
- **Compound Bows (*Taljajousi*)**: By far the most common (over 90% of Finnish bow hunters). Highly efficient, with mechanical cams providing "let-off" (holding weight is only 15–20% of full draw weight), allowing steady aiming through sight pins.
- **Traditional Bows (Recurve & Longbow / *Vastakaari- ja pitkäjousi*)**: Legal, requiring instinctive aiming and rigorous training.
- 🚫 **CROSSBOWS ARE ILLEGAL FOR HUNTING**: Under Finnish hunting law, crossbows (*varsijousi*) are strictly prohibited for hunting any game species. The bow must be drawn and held manually by human muscle power.

### 2. Legal Power Requirements
- **Minimum Draw Force (*Vetovoima*)**: The bow's peak draw weight must be at least **180 Newtons** (equivalent to **40.5 pounds / lbs**). 
- *Beginner Recommendation*: Most adult hunters choose a compound bow set between 50 and 70 lbs for flat trajectory and deep penetration.

### 3. Arrow & Broadhead Regulations
- **For Small Game** (hare, grouse, waterfowl, raccoon dog):
  - Broadheads, small-game judo points, or blunts can be used.
- **For Deer & Wild Boar** (roe deer, white-tailed deer, fallow deer, wild boar, mouflon):
  - The arrow tip must be a razor-sharp **cutting broadhead (*leikkaava kärki*)** with a cutting diameter of **at least 22 millimeters**.
  - Mechanical (expandable) broadheads and fixed-blade broadheads meeting this width are both legal.
- **For Beaver (*Majava*)**:
  - The arrow **must be tethered to the bow with a fishing line/cord (*siima*)** to retrieve the animal from the water and prevent loss.

## 🎯 The Bow Shooting Test (*Jousiampumakoe*)

Do you need an exam to hunt with a bow?
- **Small Game**: If you only hunt small game (hares, birds, foxes, raccoon dogs), you **only** need the standard Finnish Hunting Card (*metsästäjätutkinto* passed + paid game fee). No shooting test is required!
- **Ungulates & Wild Boar**: If you hunt roe deer, white-tailed deer, fallow deer, sika deer, red deer, forest reindeer, wild boar, or mouflon, you **must pass the official Bow Shooting Test**.

### Test Specifications:
- **Organizer**: Local Game Management Association (RHY).
- **Target**: A life-sized 2D target of a deer/moose with an anatomical vital zone.
- **Distance**: **18 meters**.
- **Shooting Format**:
  - **3 arrows** shot within **180 seconds (3 minutes)**.
  - All 3 arrows must strike inside the designated circular hit zone (diameter: **23 cm**).
  - Practice field tips are used during the test (you do not shoot razor broadheads into the test target).
- **Validity**: A passing certificate is valid for **3 years** and is automatically uploaded into your **Oma riista** digital profile.
- **Fee**: **20 €** per attempt.

## 🦌 What Can You Hunt with a Bow in Finland?

| Game Category | Species | Requirements |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Small Game Birds** | Capercaillie, Black Grouse, Hazel Grouse, Mallard, Pigeon | Hunting Card only |
| **Small Mammals** | European Hare, Mountain Hare, Raccoon Dog, Red Fox | Hunting Card only |
| **Semi-Aquatic** | Eurasian Beaver, Canadian Beaver | Hunting Card + Tethered Arrow |
| **Small Deer** | Roe Deer (*Metsäkauris*) | Hunting Card + Bow Test + Land Permit |
| **Medium/Large Deer** | White-tailed Deer (*Valkohäntäpeura*), Fallow Deer | Hunting Card + Bow Test + Club/Quota Permit |
| **Wild Boar** | Wild Boar (*Villisika*) | Hunting Card + Bow Test + Land Permit |

*(Note: European Moose / *hirvi* is legally restricted to firearms; bow hunting moose is not permitted in Finland).*

## 🤝 Community & Mentorship

- **Suomen Jousimetsästäjäin Liitto (SJML - Finnish Bow Hunters' Association)**:
  - Website: **[jousimetsastys.fi](https://www.jousimetsastys.fi)**
  - Runs the renowned *Jousimetsästyksen peruskurssi* (Basic Course in Bow Hunting).
  - Maintains 3D practice targets in nature trails across the country.
  - Active regional chapters organize shared hunts and deer stand rotations for new members.
